About Caspian

Caspian is a 4-year-old German Shepherd & Husky mix who started out as a foster and never left. He's whip-smart, endlessly food-motivated, and always ready for the next trail. His first hike was the start of a whole new hobby for him — and for us.

Before he was ours, Caspian spent time in a shelter and then a few months with a rescue. We fostered him to see if he'd be the right fit, and it only took a week for us to say yes. He's a very sweet boy: playful when he wants to play, and the rest of the time he relaxes and sleeps in between walks.
